# Customer Communication Template

## Acknowledge, clarify, act, and close

Hello [Customer Name],

Thank you for bringing this to our attention. I understand how [briefly acknowledge the impact] can be frustrating.

I’m reviewing the details and will [specific next action]. If this needs to be handled by another team, I’ll make sure the relevant information is shared so you do not have to start over.

I’ll update you by [realistic time or agreed update point]. Thank you for your patience.

Best regards,  
[Your Name]
