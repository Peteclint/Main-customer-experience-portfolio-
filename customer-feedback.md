# Case Study: Using Customer Feedback to Support Service Improvement

## Context

Customer feedback can reveal recurring questions, friction points, and opportunities to improve support processes.

## Relevant experience

At Kalabash Technology Solutions, I logged customer interactions and analyzed feedback using Microsoft Engage. I also tracked performance metrics and recommended improvements to boost customer satisfaction.

## Approach

1. Capture feedback accurately and retain the context of the interaction.
2. Group similar comments or recurring issues where appropriate.
3. Identify patterns that may require process clarification or internal follow-up.
4. Share observations with the relevant team.
5. Track whether an agreed improvement addresses the reported issue.

## Measures worth tracking

These are suggested measures for a future portfolio project, not historical results claimed from my employment:

- First response time
- Resolution time
- Ticket backlog and ageing
- Reopen rate
- Customer satisfaction (CSAT), where collected
- Escalation volume and recurring contact reasons

## Evidence from my CV

Customer feedback logging and analysis, performance metric tracking, and recommendations for service improvement.
