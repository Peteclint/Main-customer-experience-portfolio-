# Case Study: Escalation and Cross-Team Resolution

## Context

Some customer issues require input from teams outside the frontline support function. A good escalation should preserve context and help the receiving team act without unnecessary back-and-forth.

## Relevant experience

At Kalabash Technology Solutions, I collaborated with internal teams to escalate and resolve complex customer issues. My role also involved handling inbound and outbound calls through Switchvox and recording interactions in Freshdesk.

## Approach

1. Listen carefully and confirm the customer's desired outcome.
2. Gather relevant details and review the interaction history.
3. Explain what can be handled immediately and what requires escalation.
4. Send the receiving team a concise summary, including the issue, checks completed, and requested action.
5. Keep the customer informed without promising an unconfirmed resolution time.
6. Record the outcome and close the loop with the customer.

## What good looks like

- The customer feels heard.
- The escalation contains enough information to be actionable.
- Ownership and next steps are clear.
- The customer receives an update when there is meaningful progress.

## Evidence from my CV

Cross-functional collaboration, complex-issue escalation, Freshdesk case handling, and inbound/outbound call management.
