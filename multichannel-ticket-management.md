# Case Study: Multichannel Ticket Management

## Context

Customer questions can arrive through different channels, including email, chat, and phone. Keeping each interaction visible and properly documented helps support continuity and timely follow-up.

## Relevant experience

At Hugo Tech, I handled customer inquiries through email, chat, and phone using Zendesk, Zoho, and Intercom. I tracked and updated support tickets, documented interactions, and escalated complex issues.

## Approach

1. Review the customer's request and identify the main issue.
2. Confirm the relevant details before taking action.
3. Record the interaction in the appropriate support or CRM system.
4. Provide a clear response or route the issue to the appropriate team.
5. Update the ticket so the next person handling it can understand the history.
6. Follow up in line with the applicable support process.

## Customer experience principles

- Acknowledge the customer's concern.
- Use clear, respectful language.
- Avoid asking customers to repeat information already documented.
- Set realistic expectations about next steps.
- Keep records accurate and current.

## Evidence from my CV

Experience with Zendesk, Zoho, Intercom, ticket updates, CRM documentation, and escalation.

## Reflection

Consistent documentation and ownership help create a smoother experience when customers move between support channels.
